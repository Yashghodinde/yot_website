## SERVICE 7 — INTERNAL MAIL SERVER SETUP (NEW)

### Full Scope of Work

**The problem we solve:**
Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace charge ₹125–400/user/month. For a 10-person team, that's ₹15,000–50,000/year. With your own domain and server, you pay once for setup, then near-zero ongoing cost.

**What we set up:**
- Mail server software (Postfix + Dovecot stack OR iRedMail for easier management)
- Domain email configuration (MX records, SPF, DKIM, DMARC)
- Webmail interface (Roundcube or Rainloop) so team accesses email from browser
- SMTP/IMAP configuration guides for Outlook, Gmail, Apple Mail
- Spam filtering (SpamAssassin integration)
- Alias management (info@, support@, sales@)
- SSL encryption for all mail transmission
- Email quotas and user management panel

**Client outcome:**
- Create unlimited email addresses on their domain (contact@, ceo@, hr@, etc.)
- Zero per-user fees to Microsoft or Google
- Full ownership of email data
- Professional branded email for entire team

### Visual Presentation
```
[Headline]: "Your domain. Your emails. Your rules."

[Before/After comparison]:
Before: Paying ₹50,000/year to Microsoft for 10 users
After:  One-time setup. Unlimited emails. ₹0/month ongoing.

[What you get list]:
✓ Unlimited email addresses on your domain
✓ contact@, ceo@, team@yourbrand.com — all for free after setup
✓ Webmail + desktop + mobile access
✓ SPF/DKIM/DMARC configured (prevents spam marking)
✓ Full data ownership — not on Google/Microsoft servers
```
