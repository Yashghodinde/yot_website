- No hero section / landing section on homepage (went straight to services grid)
- No about us / team section
- No testimonials or client reviews
- No pricing page
- No animations beyond hover effects (no GSAP, Framer Motion, scroll animations)

- Contact only via Google Form (no embedded form, no live chat)
- Social proof was only portfolio items (no numbers, stats, or client logos)
- No dark/light toggle was implemented (it was in code but commented out)
